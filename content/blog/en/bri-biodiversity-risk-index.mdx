The **BRI (Biodiversity Risk Index)** assesses the ecotoxicological risk of a plant protection product for four groups of non-target organisms, sentinel species of terrestrial and aquatic ecosystems. It is the only indicator in the AGRITEMIS suite that simultaneously covers the soil, aerial and aquatic compartments.

## What the BRI measures

The BRI combines two components:

- **75%** — Active substance (sum of risk scores for the 4 groups of organisms)
- **25%** — Secondary metabolites (maximum risk among all referenced metabolites, per organism)

Each group of organisms is assessed independently by combining a toxicity score and an exposure factor.

## Breakdown by organism

### Bees

| Factor | Data used |
|---|---|
| Acute toxicity | Acute oral and contact toxicity |
| Exposure | Persistence in the crop |

### Birds

| Factor | Data used |
|---|---|
| Acute and chronic toxicity | Acute oral and chronic toxicity |
| Exposure | Persistence in soil and on the plant |

### Earthworms

| Factor | Data used |
|---|---|
| Acute and chronic toxicity | Acute and chronic toxicity (reproduction) |
| Exposure (soil) | Persistence in soil |

### Fish

| Factor | Data used |
|---|---|
| Acute and chronic toxicity | Acute and chronic toxicity |
| CLP hazard statements | H400–H413 |
| Exposure | Leaching (mobility, persistence in soil), runoff (mobility, persistence in soil) |

## PFA rule (forever pollutant)

If the active substance or one of its metabolites is a PFAS, exposure is automatically raised to its maximum for all organisms.

## Secondary metabolites

For each group of organisms, metabolites are assessed according to the same toxicity and exposure criteria as the parent substance. The BRI metabolite score = maximum risk among all referenced metabolites, per organism.

## How to read a BRI value

The BRI score reflects the cumulative pressure on biodiversity. A product with a high BRI may be very toxic to a single group of organisms or moderately harmful to several simultaneously.

| BRI level | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Very high | Severe ecotoxicity for one or more groups + high persistence |
| High | Significant risk concentrated on one compartment (e.g. aquatic only) |
| Medium | Moderate risk spread across several groups |
| Low / Very low | Rapid degradation and/or low ecotoxicity for all groups |

## Data sources

- **PPDB** (Pesticide Properties DataBase) — ecotoxicology module
- **European assessment reports** for active substances (EFSA)
- **CLP Regulation** — aquatic hazard classifications (H400–H413)

## Key difference with the EIQ

The **EIQ** aggregates biodiversity risk into a single score without distinguishing compartments. The BRI identifies **which group of organisms bears the risk** — essential information for choosing alternative products or adapting application periods to protect sensitive species.
