The **WARI (Water Risk Indicator)** is a cross-cutting indicator that combines the risks related to water contamination from two complementary angles: the **health of drinking water consumers** (via CORIwater) and the **acute and chronic toxicity for aquatic organisms** (via the FISH component of the BRI). It forms the scientific basis of the AGRITEMIS **WaterScore** product.

## What the WARI measures

**WARI = CORIwater + FISH**

The two components are calculated independently and reflect distinct concerns:

| Component | Target | Nature of risk |
|---|---|---|
| **CORIwater** | Drinking water consumers | Chronic toxicity via groundwater contamination |
| **FISH** | Aquatic environment (fish) | Acute and chronic toxicity via runoff and leaching |

## CORIwater — Risk for water consumers

### Active substance (75%)

| Factor | Data used |
|---|---|
| **Chronic toxicity** | Carcinogenicity, endocrine disruption, reproductive toxicity, neurotoxicity, genotoxicity, mitochondrial damage, chronic organ damage, CLP hazard statements of the pesticide |
| **Leaching exposure** | Mobility, persistence in soil, PFA (forever pollutant) |

### Secondary metabolites (25%)

Same toxicity and exposure criteria — maximum value taken among all metabolites (PPDB / BPDB).

## FISH — Risk for the aquatic environment

### Active substance (75%)

| Factor | Data used |
|---|---|
| **Acute and chronic toxicity** | Acute and chronic toxicity |
| **CLP hazard statements** | H400–H413 (aquatic hazard) |
| **Runoff exposure** | Mobility, persistence in soil |
| **Leaching exposure** | Mobility, persistence in soil |
| **PFA** | Automatic maximum exposure |

### Secondary metabolites (25%)

Same toxicological and exposure criteria — maximum value taken (PPDB / BPDB).

## PFA rule (forever pollutant)

For both components, if the active substance or one of its metabolites is a PFAS, all exposure factors are automatically raised to their maximum value.

## How to read a WARI value

The WARI is particularly relevant for:

- Farms located in **drinking water catchment areas** or **protection perimeters**
- Sectors subject to **environmental reporting obligations** (CSRD, Ecophyto)
- Stakeholders involved in **certification or labeling** initiatives that include water impact

| WARI level | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Very high | Mobile and/or persistent substance + strong aquatic ecotoxicity |
| High | Risk concentrated on one of the two components (groundwater or aquatic organisms) |
| Medium | Moderate risk — monitoring recommended in sensitive areas |
| Low / Very low | Low mobility, rapid degradation, limited aquatic toxicity |

## Data sources

- **PPDB** — soil, water and aquatic ecotoxicology module
- **CLP classifications** — aquatic hazard (H400–H413)
- **European approval dossiers** for active substances (EFSA)

## Key difference with existing indicators

Conventional water risk assessment methods (**SIRIS**, **CORPEN**) are based on persistence and mobility criteria, without integrating the chronic toxicological dimension for consumers. The WARI combines these two dimensions — **potential exposure + intrinsic hazard** — and incorporates the cumulative impact of the entire treatment schedule, including metabolites.
